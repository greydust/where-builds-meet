## Channel and outcome arithmetic

For each probability state, choose the physical and attribute attack rolls for each outcome. Abrasion uses minimum attack, normal and critical use the midpoint, and Affinity uses maximum. T6 浮梁 forces 鳴戈 to Affinity. 載戈 uses its current outcome distribution. Red rank 21 scales both no-flat coefficients by 1.0725, yielding **0.29601** for 載戈 and **1.9305** for 鳴戈. Both flat damage terms are zero, so a Bonus Attack modifier on the context cannot add flat damage.

1. Physical base = coefficient × (effective Physical Attack roll × attack multiplier − adjusted enemy defense). Multiply by Physical penetration and Physical channel damage.
2. Bellstrike base = attribute coefficient × Bellstrike Attack roll × attribute attack multiplier. Multiply by Bellstrike penetration, Bellstrike channel damage and the 1.5 main-path multiplier. These two channels account for all damage in this trace.
3. Each channel receives the applicable shared product: (1 + 鋒銳 base ratio) × (1 + NPC correction) × (1 + Category 1 bonuses) × (1 + specific-skill attunement). The attribute path also includes any attribute-only Category 1 bonus. The trace records the physical and attribute products individually.
4. Apply the outcome multiplier (1 for normal or abrasion, 1 + critical or Affinity damage bonus for the respective outcome), any DOT multiplier and the global damage multiplier. Sum channels. Average the four outcomes with their own probabilities, then average the resulting states with their recorded weights. Healing from leech is separate.

Penetration uses 1 + (penetration − resistance) / 200 when penetration meets or exceeds resistance, otherwise 1 + (penetration − resistance) / 100. Defense subtraction and penetration are distinct operations.

## Using the trace

Open hits in time order. Each contact includes the authored action, deterministic buffs/debuffs, expected stacks where applicable, effects and production breakdown. Each leaf supplies its probability weight, resolved modifiers, penetration and channel factors, attack ranges, outcome rates, and four channel vectors in abrasion/normal/critical/Affinity order. The vectors include their outcome damage multipliers already. For each leaf, sum each vector's channels, multiply by its outcome probability, sum outcomes, then multiply by leaf weight. Sum leaves for the contact. This reconstruction was checked against all 80 production contact totals within 0.000001 damage. These checks establish what the simulator applied. The CN audit should independently verify eligibility, effective stack snapshots and multiplier placement.
