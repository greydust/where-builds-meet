# Bolt Solo 25 damage eligibility audit

This report describes the current calculator's **Dummy 1 Min** rotation with the supplied tier-115 gear, four pieces of 寒芒, rank-21 red and blue spears, and all four equipped Inner Ways at T6. The target has 687 Physical Defense and 180% Judgement Resistance. Food contributes 240–480 effective Physical Attack. Fire Divinecraft is selected. This is a map of modeled eligibility. Actual buff coverage depends on the individual contact.

## How to read the skill rows

- **Attack inputs** include raw, sheet and effective attack. Both arts supply Bellstrike attack. Red converts Momentum to maximum Physical Attack and maximum Bellstrike Attack to Bellstrike Penetration. Blue converts Momentum to Affinity and maximum Bellstrike Attack to Bellstrike channel damage. Food enters effective attack after these sheet conversions.
- **Bonus Attack** scales a skill's flat attack component when the wielded spear matches the talent and the hit carries Bolt. The red no-flat talent instead scales the coefficients of hits tagged BoltNoFlat, including while wielding blue. It applies once.
- **Channel damage** applies separately to Physical and Bellstrike damage. 泉威 grants both bonuses to its applier, with an extra increment for the VC series. The blue talent grants both while 鋒銳 is active **or** the hit is 鳴戈. Penetration acts separately on each eligible channel.
- **Category 1 (event damage)** adds applicable HP damage bonuses, including 寒芒 four-piece, boss damage, applicable weapon and martial-art bonuses, and 月相 or 月引. **Outcome** effects change hit probabilities or the damage of the selected outcome. 寒芒's Varied Combo bonus raises Affinity damage specifically. 王行, 月引 and 暗潮 can add Direct Affinity.
- **Event 34 base ratio**, **NPC correction** and **specific-skill attunement** are separate factors. For a T6 鋒銳 鳴戈 against the modeled monster, 鋒銳 × NPC correction gives 1.30 × 1.60 = 2.08×. The four VC attunements add to 42.4% (4 × 10.6%), giving a single factor of 1.424. Together these three factors give **2.08 × 1.424 = 2.96192×**. Each active 載戈 stack adds 10 percentage points to Category 1. At 15 stacks the product including that bracket is **2.96192 × (1 + 1.50 + other Category 1 bonuses)**. With zero other Category 1 bonuses this gives **2.96192 × 2.50 = 7.4048×**, before the remaining damage factors. 月湧 T6 DAMAGE_UP adds inside the NPC-correction bracket for its two Moon skills.

## Audit boundaries

The calculator settles ordinary early-Tab 鳴戈 against current stacks before clearing them. A competing queued hit can defer native processing by 1 ms. The in-game queue state at the four screenshot detonations is unknown. 鳴戈已引爆 is a once-per-window marker. It changes trigger count only.

The expected-value Moon model extends 月引 by expected Affinity probability per hit, uses a whole-second Tide clock and promotes very small aggregate 月相 probabilities. Buff timing and skill eligibility should be audited separately. The authored Qi/exhaust timeline also affects refill opportunity and the buffs present at individual contacts.
